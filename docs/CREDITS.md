# Credits

All third-party art, sound and music are **CC0 (public domain)**. No attribution is legally required, but we credit them anyway.

## Art
- **Pixel-boy, "Ninja Adventure" asset pack** (CC0): pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack
  Characters (the Knight hero, Evil You, the Stranger, robots), monsters (eyes, octopuses, T-Rex raptors, bear), bosses (squid, cyclops, giant beast, segmented dragon), tilesets, props, effects (slashes, explosions, smoke, sparks, magic), particles, the pixel font and UI pieces.
  Recolored per era and assembled by `tools/asset_scripts/build_ninja_assets.py`.
- **Kenney, 1-Bit Pack** (CC0): kenney.nl. Artifact icons and the old placeholder set.

## Sound effects
- **Pixel-boy, Ninja Adventure** (CC0): hits, impacts, explosions, coins, power-ups, heals, menu sounds, ambience (rain, wind, river).
- **Kenney** Impact / Sci-fi / Interface / RPG audio (CC0): wood and glass breaking, footsteps, lasers, metal, glitch, chest latch.
- **Synthesized for the jam** (`tools/asset_scripts/build_audio.py`): the perfect-deflect sound, sword draw, swing whooshes, spin, crit, combo ticks, heartbeat, boss roar, portal, dash.

## Music

## Voices
- Generated offline with Windows' built-in text-to-speech voices (Microsoft David / Zira) and processed per character
  (`tools/asset_scripts/make_voices.ps1` + `process_voices.py`). Lines are listed in `tools/asset_scripts/voice_lines.json`.
